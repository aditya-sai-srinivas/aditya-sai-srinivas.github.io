Aditya Sai Srinivas

Computer Vision Engineer — 3D Reconstruction & Differentiable Rendering

I build the software that turns raw multi-camera and multi-projector capture into geometry a business can rely on. For the past three and a half years I've been the founding engineer on the 3D reconstruction pipeline at Allsides GmbH — calibration, correspondence, differentiable rendering, and everything in between — shipped to clients including Amazon, Meta, Nike, and Zalando across two hardware generations.

I'm not a research scientist by title. I don't have a publication record. What I have instead is a few years of taking hard 3D vision problems all the way to something that runs unsupervised, at scale, at real client sites — and a habit of writing up the things I learn along the way, including the ones that didn't work.

Currently

Industrializing camera and projector calibration across a 30-camera, 9-projector light stage to sub-0.2mm reprojection accuracy; developing a Gaussian ray tracing prototype inside a differentiable rendering framework (Mitsuba 3, DrJIT); guiding two PhD interns on sim-ready 3D asset problems, including encoding mechanical material properties directly into reconstructed geometry.

Background

M.Sc. Computational Science and Engineering, Technical University of Munich — thesis on shape correspondence via learnable pose canonicalization, supervised by Prof. Daniel Cremers. B.E. Mechanical Engineering, National Institute of Engineering, Mysuru.
