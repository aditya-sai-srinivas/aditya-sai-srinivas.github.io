Projects

Production work and things built outside of it

ALLSIDES GMBH · 2023–PRESENT

Multi-Camera / Multi-Projector Reconstruction Pipeline

The core geometry reconstruction system I architect and maintain as founding engineer: raw sensor data from 30 cameras and 9 projectors, through custom calibration and a distorted camera model, to point clouds and production geometry at sub-0.2mm reprojection accuracy. Shipped across two hardware generations to Amazon, Meta, Adidas, Nike, and others.

PERSONAL · 2024–PRESENT

Divergence Free Correspondence

A C++ library for velocity-field-based 3D point cloud deformation, with parallel correspondence computation. Uses SHOT feature extraction and sparse matrix correspondence, with a nonlinear least-squares optimization pipeline built on Ceres Solver.

OPEN SOURCE · rohan-sawhney/zombie

Zombie — Monte Carlo Geometry Processing

An open-source framework for estimating geometric and physical properties of 3D assets by solving PDEs in a mesh-free setting (the Walk-on-Spheres line of work). My contribution was building out the CI and testing infrastructure — see the writing page for why the underlying method interests me.
